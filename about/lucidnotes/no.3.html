However i had an idea of what was there inside it, because i think about it very often, and sometimes i obsesses over my past, those days were my prime, I was socially active, and i was perceived as some “big dawg” guy at the class, I atleast had a decent physique not soo fit.

I loved music and i took guitar classes, I thought id be a musician/pop star like Justin Bieber. That was the time I discovered a bunch of music that were niche in India.

And the list goes on, I am truly fascinated by the fact that I’ve discovered these music and these artists in 2017 that was way before the brain rot social media algorithm existed. I was passionated, ambitious and disciplined. Today I can barely move, both mentally and physically. What a disgrace have I done to myself. The past obsession is one of the worst sufferings, imagine being betrayed by none other than the whole self, imagine being in a cycle of a destructive routine - realisation - acceptance and getting back to the routine. Maybe is it the fight between the conscious vs subconscious?! I really don’t know. However I am the Man now that’s way ahead intellectually, ideologically comparing to the boy I used to be. Maybe as they say the more i started to know the less the life gets interesting?!. The memes and the internet culture that keeps me alive by end of the day. So fuck it we ball.
